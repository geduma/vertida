# Vertida · coffee recipes and timer

Vertida helps you brew specialty coffee at home without friction: pick your method, set your dose, and follow the step-by-step timer until you serve.

## What you can do

- Choose from 4 methods: V60, Chemex, Aeropress, and French press.
- Calculate your coffee from the water you want to use.
- Follow classic phase-guided recipes with a timer.
- Discover unusual recipes or get a random surprise pick.
- Create your own homemade recipes and mark favorites.
- Use it in Spanish or English, with light, dark, or automatic mode.
- Install it on your phone and use it offline in the kitchen.

## Privacy

Your recipes, favorites, and settings stay only on your device. No account needed to use it.
