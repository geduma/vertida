# Brew validation checklist

> **Status: not executed.** This document is a protocol for validating the 40 classic and 18 discovery recipes against real brews. It is not evidence that the catalog has been validated.

Vertida's recipes are curated, not lab-tested. The goal is to confirm that each recipe is brewable at home, that times and ratios produce a drinkable cup, and that the tags describe what the cup actually tastes like.

## How to run a session

1. Use a 0.1 g scale, a timer and the method's suggested grind (table header). Keep water at the method temperature when defined.

2. Brew each recipe **three times**: with the reference dose, one cup up, and one cup down (skip if the method has a single cup).

3. Score each trial 1–5 for **balance**, **clarity/sweetness** and **agreement with the listed tags**. Add free notes for channeling, stall, bitterness or sourness.

4. Acceptance: average **≥ 4** across the three trials, no blocked/stalled brew, and total time within **±10%** of the reference. Anything below goes back to the recipe table with a proposed ratio, dose or phase change.

5. Record the grinder, water, coffee and roast date at the top of each method section; results are only comparable within the same setup.
